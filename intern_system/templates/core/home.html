Malawi Police Headquarters Intern Management

Building safer career pathways for interns at Malawi Police Headquarters.

A modern, secure platform for police officers, school supervisors, and interns to access real-time performance tracking, attendance management, and weekly reporting.

About Malawi Police Headquarters

Malawi Police Headquarters leads the nation's law enforcement through transparency, discipline, and community engagement. This platform supports the headquarters' mission by helping shape the next generation of officers and civic leaders.

Community Impact

Empowering recruits and interns with real-world police placements that reinforce public service values and accountability.

Trust & Integrity

Designed to support the police force's commitment to ethical operations, clear reporting, and structured supervision.

For Police Officers

Police supervisors can manage intern assignments, review weekly performance updates, and approve reports from a centralized dashboard. It provides the visibility needed to mentor interns effectively.

Weekly Reports

Generate and download professional intern reports for auditing and review.

Performance Insights

Compare attendance and performance trends to support targeted coaching and supervision.

For School Supervisors

School supervisors can access student records and monitor internship progress in real time, enabling faster approvals, better communication, and stronger collaboration with police mentors.

Student Tracking

See which students are placed, their supervisor assignments, and current internship status.

Secure Communication

Coordinate with police supervisors and stay informed about student development seamlessly.

For Interns

Interns can log in to submit Application, review their attendance, and receive feedback. This platform makes the internship experience more transparent, structured, and rewarding.

Internship Access

View your assignments, report progress, and monitor your development from one dashboard.

Success Tracking

Track your progress with clear goals and measurable results through weekly performance summaries.
